# wif5 part a: faults seen by a fresh viewer (frames 0-150 clip + stills 0/60/150/300)

| id | frame | what a viewer sees wrong | severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | 0-150, 300 | The dad never looks up at the clock. His face points at the camera the whole time; at 300 only the eyes drift a little. The clock is a small dial in the top-left corner, far from him, so the "midnight nears" beat does not come across with the sound off. | blocks |
| F2 | 0-150 | Nothing tells a muted viewer this is a New Year countdown. The clock hands sit at about 11:58-12:00 but the dial is small (about 150 px) and partly hidden by bunting from frame 150 on. There are no fireworks, no crowd, no number. With the tree and market stalls it reads as Christmas. | blocks |
| F3 | 0-150 | The square is empty: one figure, no crowd, no other people. That is not a festive midnight. The set feels like a closed street. | visible |
| F4 | 0-150, 300 | The bottom 25-30 % of the frame is near-black cobblestone (mean luma 10-12 of 255). Wasted space, and it makes the frame feel dark rather than warm. | visible |
| F5 | 0-150, 300 | The figure stands in the band from y 670 to 1000, which is the middle band where the text will go. A caption would cover his face. The top 40 % is empty sky. | visible |
| F6 | 110-130 | During the cheer jump the thin black legs disappear against the dark navy buildings and ground. He looks like a floating torso with no feet, and you cannot tell where he will land. | visible |
| F7 | 0-60 | The opening barely moves. For about 2 s he stands with one small arm wave and a blink; only the snow moves. The frame-0 pose is a neutral stand, so the hook is weak. | visible |
| F8 | 0-300 | The head is a flat, near-white disc (luma about 160-205, the brightest large object in frame) with no shading. It looks pasted on and draws the eye away from the face marks. | visible |
| F9 | 100-105, 140-146 | In the crouch, and in the beat after the jump, the face slides to the bottom of the head and reads squashed. At about 144 the mouth is a thin line and the face is hard to read. | minor |
| F10 | 0-150 | The figure is small (head about 100 px wide in 720) and placed off-centre in front of a busy tree. His sweater and tree lights compete, so the face is easy to lose at phone size. | minor |
| F11 | 150, 300 | At 300 the camera has pushed in and bunting crosses right over the clock face. The one prop the story needs is half hidden. | minor |
| F12 | 0-300 | Warmth comes only from the lamps and stall awnings. Most of the frame is cold navy and black, so the look is "cold night", not "warm and rich". | minor |
