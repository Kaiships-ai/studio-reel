# Restaging

## Overview

How the series shows a real place and a real person with what the kit has: the troupe plays the person, a Halden set plays the place, and one printed thing in the world names the real place and year.

## Rules & Limitations

- One printed label per place change states where and when ("FREMANTLE HOSPITAL · 1984" on the board, "STOCKHOLM · 2005" on a banner). The reference prints its facts on posters, cans and newspapers (FORENSIC.md section 5, detail 2).
- A real place may borrow any set whose shape fits (a lecture room plays a conference). Never claim a set looks like the real place.
- The troupe keeps its identity marks; a role adds one piece (tie, badge, hand prop).
- Real objects that carry the fact (the beaker, the rubber tank, the jackscrew) are modelled as props with print, so the camera can insert on them.

## History

Chosen for season 1 so episodes swap scripts, not worlds.
