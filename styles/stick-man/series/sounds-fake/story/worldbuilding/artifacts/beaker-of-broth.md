# Beaker of Broth

## Description

A small glass beaker of murky amber beef broth, with two scraped petri dishes beside it. Hand prop with a grip socket; printed label "BEEF BROTH". The episode's motif prop (seen at l01, l07, l14 as the medal reflects in it on the shelf).

## Function

Carries the hook (l01) and the act (l07).
