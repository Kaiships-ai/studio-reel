# Ep03 Raising Chicago fact check

## Question

Did Chicago lift whole buildings, with people inside, to raise its streets, and by how much?

## Findings

| Claim | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| City on swampy ground; 1854 cholera killed 6 % of the population; 1856 Chesbrough sewer plan; streets raised and buildings jacked up in the 1850s-60s | Raising of Chicago (Overview, citing Chicago Daily Tribune 1854) | medium |
| 1860: half a block of Lake Street (~320 ft, est. 35,000 tons) raised 4 ft 8 in in five days by 600 men with 6,000 jackscrews; shops stayed open | Raising of Chicago (citing Chicago Daily Press and Tribune, Mar.-Apr. 1860) | medium |
| 1861: Ely, Smith and Pullman raised the Tremont House six feet | Tremont House (Chicago) (citing Tribune 22 Jan 1861); Raising of Chicago | medium |
| Tremont House: "500 men ... 1,000 jackscrews"; guests "oblivious"; a patron found the steps steeper and, at checkout, the windows several feet above his head | Raising of Chicago (citing Tribune 26 Feb 1861; Macrae 1870) | low: other accounts give 1,200 men / 5,000 screws, or 1,584 screws |
