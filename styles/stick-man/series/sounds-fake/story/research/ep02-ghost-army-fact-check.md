# Ep02 Ghost Army fact check

## Question

Is the "four GIs pick up a tank" story true, and what are the unit's checkable numbers?

## Findings

| Claim | Source and exact wording | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Two Frenchmen on bicycles saw four GIs pick up a Sherman tank; guard told them "The Americans are very strong." | Works That Work: Cpl. Arthur Shilstone "stopped two Frenchmen on bicycles"; they saw "four GIs picking up a 40-ton Sherman tank and turning it around"; Shilstone: "The Americans are very strong." | medium (one secondary source opened; Smithsonian has the same anecdote but returned 403) |
| rubber M-4s weighed 93 pounds inflated; officers had commanded 32-ton tanks | ghostarmy.com quoting the official history: "Officers who had once commanded 32-ton tanks, felt frustrated and helpless with a battalion of rubber M-4s, 93 pounds fully inflated." | high (primary quoted) |
| unit: 23rd Headquarters Special Troops, about 1,100 men | Wikipedia: "The 1,100-man unit"; Works That Work: 1,100 men | high |
| sound played from speakers on halftracks, audible 15 miles away | Wikipedia: "These sounds were audible 15 miles (24 km) away." | medium |
| more than 20 deception campaigns; secret until declassified in 1996 | Wikipedia | medium |
| artists incl. Bill Blass, Ellsworth Kelly | Wikipedia | medium |

## Search Plan

Before the script: open the Smithsonian piece in a browser, Rick Beyer's book or the PBS film page for the Shilstone anecdote (second source), and the official history for the 1,100 figure.

## Story Use

Say "a Sherman tank" (no tonnage): the anecdote's "40-ton" is the witnesses' belief; the official history says 32-ton. Status stays `open` until the second source for the anecdote is opened.
