# Chapter 3: The Hotel That Went Up

## Outline

1. Hook: a city lifts a hotel six feet into the air with the guests still inside.
2. Setup: 1850s Chicago sits in mud; cholera; the 1856 sewer plan means the streets must go up.
3. Doubt: you can't raise the street without raising every building on it (Bram).
4. Ladder: 1860, half a block of Lake Street, 600 men, 6,000 jackscrews, 4 ft 8 in in five days, shops open.
5. Turn: 1861, the Tremont House; Pullman's crew under the floor turning screws in time.
6. "You see,": each man turns his screws a little at a time on a signal, so the building rises evenly.
7. Payoff: the guest who found the front steps steeper checks out and the windows are above his head.
