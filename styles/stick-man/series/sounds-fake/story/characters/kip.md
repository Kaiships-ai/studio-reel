# Kip

## Appearance

Actor name **Kip** (never "Kai", so the actor never collides with Kai the owner); kit spec stays `KAI` (`engine/cast.js`), never changed: white head and shirt, brown curls with a red bandana knot (bushy, red-topped silhouette), round glasses, orange vest with a green strap. One role piece per episode may be added on top (a tie, a hand prop); the curls, bandana, glasses and vest stay.

## Personality & Traits

Curious, cocky, impatient with "that can't work". Commits with the whole body. Never angry; when it goes wrong he looks surprised, then tries again.

## Backstory

Troupe actor. Plays the person who does the odd thing in each true story (the doctor who drinks the bug, the guard who says "very strong", the man who lifts a hotel).

## Motivations & Goals

Wants proof. Needs an audience that laughed first.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

He does not speak; the narrator quotes the real person he plays. Body voice: points, presents, flings arms wide, lifts heavy things, celebrates.

## Signature Poses

`point_lean`, `present`, `arms_wide`, `lift_heavy`, `celebrate` (kit `engine/poses.js`).

## Face Range

excited, sly, smug, proud, grin, nervous, pain (never angry or furious).

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** laughed at (every episode)
- **Key turning points:** does the odd thing; pays a cost
- **Ending state:** proven right on the last word (flat arc: he never changes, the world does)
