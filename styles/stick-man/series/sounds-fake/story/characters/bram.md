# Bram

## Appearance

Kit spec `BRAM` (`lib/cast/extras.js`): navy-black shirt (the darkest figure on screen, reads as a black cut-out at thumbnail size), a flat dark hair cap with a pale grey band (role-independent, part of his identity) and a cool rim light on dark sets, so the navy-black never vanishes. Empty hands so the arms can cross. One role piece per episode (a tie, a cap badge) may be added.

## Personality & Traits

Sure of himself, sensible, loud laugher. Speaks for "everyone knows". Not a villain: he is the audience's common sense, and he is wrong on the last word.

## Backstory

Troupe actor. Plays the doubters: the experts, the officers, the crowd that laughs.

## Motivations & Goals

Wants the world to stay sensible. The running gag: he is wrong again, and the last frame shows his face.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Does not speak; the narrator gives the doubt ("The experts laugh."). Body voice: arms crossed, doubled-over laugh, fists on hips, facepalm, leaning away.

## Signature Poses

`arms_crossed`, `laugh`, `hands_on_hips`, `facepalm`, `lean_away`.

## Face Range

deadpan, smug, suspicious, closed (laughing), disgust, then shock on the payoff.

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** laughing
- **Key turning points:** the odd thing works
- **Ending state:** jaw dropped in the last frame (the series sign-off)
