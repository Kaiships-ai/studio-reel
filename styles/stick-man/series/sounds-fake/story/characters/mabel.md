# Mabel

## Appearance

Kit spec `MABEL` (`lib/cast/extras.js`): lilac shirt, teal vest, a wide straw sun hat with a navy ribbon (the widest silhouette in the troupe). While Mabel is a main, the kit's `WREN` (also a sun hat) is not cast, so the hat stays hers.

## Personality & Traits

Notices things first: a smell, a noise, steps that got steeper. Practical, a little nosy, quick to react with the whole body.

## Backstory

Troupe actor. Plays the person the odd thing happens to or near: the spouse, the passer-by, the hotel guest.

## Motivations & Goals

Wants to know what is going on. She is the viewer's eyes inside the story.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Does not speak; the narrator reports what she notices. Body voice: shades her eyes, cups an ear, leans in to whisper, recoils, flails.

## Signature Poses

`shade_eyes`, `cup_ear`, `lean_in`, `recoil`, `flail`.

## Face Range

suspicious, confused, worried, disgust, shock, happy, blush.

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** curious
- **Key turning points:** sees the odd thing up close
- **Ending state:** the first to believe
