# Narrator

## Appearance

None. Voice only: Gemini TTS, voice Achird, one take per episode, 2.9-3.3 words/s.

## Personality & Traits

A friend telling you the best true story they heard this week. Fast, dry, delighted by the absurd, kind to real people.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

- Hook = the odd act as a plain fact: "To win an argument, a doctor drank a beaker of live bacteria."
- Doubt is reported flatly: "The experts laugh."
- The pivot always starts "You see," and explains one hidden rule.
- Quotes the real person once per episode, short, verbatim, from a source.
- Never asks the viewer to like, follow or comment; never says "in this video".

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** knows the ending
- **Key turning points:** hides it until the last word
- **Ending state:** lands the climax on the last word, then stops
