# Laughed At

The season arc (serial `season-goal`): every episode, an idea the world laughs at turns out true. Kai does it, Bram laughs, Mabel sees it first, the last word proves it.

## Setup

Each episode opens on the odd act already happening (frame 0) and states it as a plain fact.

## Rising Action

1. The common belief, and the doubter mocks the idea (staged laugh, a rejection, a quote).
2. The odd act, climbing a ladder of 2-3 rungs (piglets -> himself; one street -> a whole hotel).
3. A cost or a surprise ("Wait.").

## Climax

"You see," names the hidden rule; the proof lands on the last word with the biggest image.

## Resolution

Bram's face in the last frame (series sign-off). Nothing carries to the next episode.
