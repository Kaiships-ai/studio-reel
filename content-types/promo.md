# Promo

**Status: new.** No finished reel has proven this shape yet; follow it closely and tell the user it is new.

## When to use

The idea is to sell or announce ONE thing the user makes: a product, a course, a template, a launch, a drop, an
offer with a date. The viewer should finish knowing what it is, who it is for, the one result it gives, and how to
get it. A reel about several products is a [listicle](listicle.md); a reel teaching how to use the product is
[education](education.md).

Before writing, the user must have given (or `workspace/brand.md` must hold): the product's name, the one result it
gives, and the offer (price, date, bonus or keyword). In `--mode cook`, ask for these once if they are missing:
they are facts only the user has. Never invent a price, a discount, a deadline, a number of customers or a review.

## Beats

| Beat | 30 s | 45 s | 60 s | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 s | 0–3 s | 0–3 s | The pain or the wish, said to the viewer ("Still editing reels for three hours?"), already moving on frame 0 |
| Problem | 3–8 s | 3–10 s | 3–12 s | One concrete picture of the pain, the viewer's own day, not a statistic |
| Reveal | 8–12 s | 10–15 s | 12–18 s | The product appears, named on screen as it is named in the voice; the style's biggest move goes here |
| Proof | 12–24 s | 15–38 s | 18–52 s | Two or three things it does, each shown working (a before and after, a screen, the thing in use), one line each |
| CTA | 24–30 s | 38–45 s | 52–60 s | The offer and one action ("Comment LAUNCH and I'll send you the early price") |

- **Show, then name.** Each proof beat shows the result first, then says what made it.
- **The product looks the same every time it appears**: one silhouette, one colour, one label. Build it once in the
  film's code and reuse it.
- **One offer.** A price, a date or a bonus, not all three. If there is a deadline, it is on screen in the CTA.
- Claims stay the user's own: "it saves me two hours a week" is fine when the user said it; "the best tool on the
  market" is not.

## Hook patterns

- "Still <painful task>? I built <product> for exactly that."
- "This is <product>, and it does <one result> in <real time>."
- "<Date>: <product> opens, and here is what you get."

## CTA rule

The keyword names the product or the launch ("Comment LAUNCH"). The viewer gets the link, the early price or the
waitlist place. One action only; no "follow, like and share".

## Styles that suit it

Materials & 3D (the product as an object: Glass Product Render, Brick Toy, Paper Pop-up Book), Information &
Keynote (software: Dark Tech Keynote, Living Screencast), Graphic & Type (a bold launch card: Swiss Motion Graphics,
60s Spy Title Sequence), Print & Printmaking (a poster drop: Silkscreen Travel Poster, Risograph Print).

## A worked outline: "launching a reel template pack" (about 35 s)

1. Hook: "Still building every reel from a blank page?" An empty timeline scrolls past on frame 0.
2. Problem: a clock spins over a half-finished edit; one line on the evening it eats.
3. Reveal: "This is <product>." The pack box lands centre frame and opens.
4. Proof: three templates fly out, each snapping onto a phone with a finished reel; one line each on what it does.
5. CTA: "It opens <date>. Comment PACK and I'll send you the early price." The date stays on screen.
