# Stick-man "thử góc máy" (camera previs from the film's own engine)

`styles/stick-man/tools/camtry.mjs` takes a director's camera note in plain words and finds the film, segment, take,
frame window and camera anchors it means. It renders 3-4 camera variants through the film's own engine (headless
Chrome, real GPU) and writes the clip, a top view, wall numbers and a stage-page section. `pick` writes the chosen
variant into the anchors and regenerates the act. The word logic is pure: `camtry-core.mjs`. The page is
`camtry.html` + `camtry-page.js`.

## Sub-features

- Resolve: the film is the newest in `films/` with a `reel.js` and its own `storyboard.json` or `<round>/gen/` (a
  segment test folder has neither), unless the note names one. The segments come from `<round>/gen/<SEG>.js` heads
  (`FRAMES` end exclusive, `CUTS`, `STAGES` clamped to the segment), else from storyboard scenes. A take is a run
  between cuts. A concept table (đua xe, ngân hàng, mở đầu, kết, súng, núp, ...) matches the segment, an ordinal
  ("thứ hai") picks the n-th named stage, and the window is the take from that stage, at most `--max` (100) frames.
  The first output line is the readback "Em hiểu là: …".
- Variants from words: up ("nâng", "thấp quá"), down ("hạ", "cao quá"), away ("ra xa", "lùi", "dính tường", "gần
  quá"), closer ("gần lại", "xa quá"), hero aim ("nhìn vào xe"). "chút" is a 0.5 m step, "nhiều" 1.5 m, "2 m" sets it.
  V0 is always the film camera. A note that names a wall moves off the nearest wall, else back from the aim. `aim:
  'lift'` raises the aim with the camera (what an anchor's `dz` does).
- Render: the engine's `stage.render` is wrapped. Each variant swaps the lens position and aim for the frame and puts
  the film camera back. V0 renders untouched (keeps its roll). All film passes run.
- Numbers per variant (definitions in `camtry.json`): wall-touch frames, wall-fill frames, the nearest wall, mean
  camera height, the lead's screen height, the facade share, and the close walls by mesh name.
- Top view: static set meshes drawn orthographic from above (taller is lighter). Yellow is the camera path, red dots
  are wall touches, green is the lead's path.
- Pick: `applyPick` adds the variant's `up` to `dz` on every anchor window the range touches (whole windows, so no
  pop). It refuses away, closer, hero aim and a tilt that holds the aim, because `dz` lifts the aim too.
  `formatSpec` writes one anchor per line. Every `<round>/gen/*.js` whose `// generated by` line names that anchors
  file is regenerated.

## How to get to it (user POV)

The "Mode: thử góc máy" section of the make-reel-stick-man skill. Kai's note goes in word for word. The section goes on
the stage page, and Kai picks there.

## Driving it

- `node styles/stick-man/tools/camtry.mjs "đoạn đua xe, máy quay dính tường nhà, nâng lên chút" --dry 1` prints the
  readback and the variants and writes `camtry.json`, in a second, with no render.
- Without `--dry`, on Hot Money ep01 r5 (worktree `hm-r5b`, CHASE take 1, 1347-1446, 4 variants, 540x960), the
  engine is ready in 2 s and the run takes about 65 s. Look for 1896 static meshes and 258 buildings, and no wall touch
  in any variant. The nearest wall is 8.93 m (V0), 9.09 m (V1, V2) and 9.6 m (V3), and the camera height is 1.07 m
  plus each lift. V0's numbers equal the sim prototype's for the same range. The recommendation is V1.
- `node styles/stick-man/tools/camtry.mjs pick <out> V1 --dry 1` prints the dz change. Without `--dry`, it writes the
  anchors file and reruns `r5/tools/xfer.mjs CHASE` and `r5/xfer/chase-extra.mjs`.
- Regression: `node --test test/unit/stick-camtry.test.mjs` (fixture `test/fixtures/camtry/library/`: the r5 act heads,
  the CHASE anchors, the CHASE test folder).

## Gotchas

- Only static set meshes count as walls. Cars, cast, instanced and skinned meshes are left out.
- A re-aimed variant drops the film camera's roll. Shadows and lens focus still follow the film camera.
- The render skips the bible check: it is a preview, never a film output.
- On Kai's Mac every run goes through the shared render lock.
